browserkit

Your real Chrome, for your agent: its tabs and your logins, visibly.

1

Run this once in a terminal

It registers the bridge and installs the browserkit skill for your agents.

npx browserkit setup
Waiting for the bridge…
2

Tell your agent to use browserkit

Add the MCP server to your agent's configuration, or just point it at the skill.

"browserkit": { "command": "npx", "args": ["browserkit", "mcp"] }

The status light turns green when the bridge answers. Everything runs in your browser, signed in as you; an agent can never reach a site you blocked.
